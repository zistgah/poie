# Code of Conduct

This repository preserves a constitutional artifact. Engagement here — issues,
discussion, infrastructure contributions — is expected to be scholarly,
respectful, and precise.

Because the Proclamation itself declares the equity of every individual mind and
idiolect, conduct in this space is held to that same standard: no participant's
identity, expression, or manner of speech is grounds for exclusion or contempt.

Reports of unacceptable conduct may be directed to the maintainer via the
contact in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
