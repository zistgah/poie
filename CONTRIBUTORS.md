# Contributors

The **text of the Proclamation** is a closed, single-author constitutional
artifact and is not open to contribution, revision, or co-authorship. See
[GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md).

Contributions to the **surrounding repository infrastructure** (packaging,
verification scripts, documentation site, metadata tooling) may be acknowledged
here. Infrastructure contributions must never alter the canonical text, its
wording, or its provenance record.
