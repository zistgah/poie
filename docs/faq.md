# FAQ

**What is this repository?**
The archival home of the Proclamation of Individual Equity, preserved as a
fixed, citable, independently verifiable constitutional artifact.

**Can I quote it?**
Yes — verbatim reproduction is permitted and encouraged under CC BY-ND 4.0 (content; per-category — see LICENSE).
Distributing *altered* wording is not.

**How do I cite it?**
Use `CITATION.cff`. Once the Zenodo DOI is minted it becomes the preferred
citation identifier.

**How do I verify it hasn't been tampered with?**
Run `sha256sum -c metadata/SHA256SUMS` from `proclamation/`, and verify the
OpenTimestamps proofs with the `ots` client. See `SECURITY.md`.

**Why CC BY-ND rather than an open-source software licence?**
This is a text artifact, not software. NoDerivatives preserves the integrity of
the wording while still allowing free verbatim distribution. (Licence choice is
the author's and may be revisited before first release.)

**Is this part of Zistgah / ILM / PRATIK?**
It is a **standalone** artifact that may be **registered within** the Zistgah
envelope by reference. It is not absorbed into, or governed by, those systems.
