# Endorse the Proclamation — signatory campaign

Individuals and institutions can place a **decentralised, cryptographically
verifiable signature** beside this Proclamation using OpenTimestamps. A signature
is a timestamp anchored to a public blockchain showing you endorsed the exact,
immutable text — no central authority issues or can revoke it.

- **Sign:** https://project-ilm.github.io/tok-doi/sign-pie.html
- **Public registry of signatures:** https://github.com/project-ilm/tok-doi/blob/main/registry/pie/signatures.jsonl
- **Signing infrastructure (Tok DOI):** https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21402745

## This is not anti-institution
Signing is an act of **preservation of individuality in the AGI era** — not a
petition, demand, or opposition to any institution. Anonymity and pseudonyms are
permitted; institutions may sign as institutions. Full statement of intent:
the Tok DOI MANIFESTO (https://github.com/project-ilm/tok-doi/blob/main/MANIFESTO.md).

> The signing page and registry are hosted by **project-ilm/tok-doi** (the atomic
> provenance layer). GitHub Pages must be enabled on that repo for the sign link
> to resolve.
