PROCLAMATION
OF
INDIVIDUAL EQUITY

IT IS HEREBY DECLARED AND AFFIRMED
that the struggle against the vestiges of cognitive domination is not a pursuit of mere historical grievance, but a rigorous reclamation of the Equitable Mind.

WHEREAS
systems subjected to domination have experienced the domination of language and culture, the remediation of this condition cannot be achieved by mere political reform, nor by the replacement of one structure with another. It must be executed through the sustainable equitisation of the tools of thought, expression, and Essence of Individuality, shifting the paradigm toward the Individual Equity of those committed to the Human Collective.

BE IT KNOWN
that the path forward is not found in the forceful impositions of conformity—which often serve to replicate the structures they seek to replace—nor in the flattening metrics that attempt to define normality, which facilitate the suppression of the individuality of minds, while maintaining Balance of Interpretation.

THE MANDATE OF EQUITY
resides exclusively at the level of the individual identity, It finds its truest expression in the idiolect—the unique, irreducible, and indisputable linguistic fingerprint of the self. Cultural and Linguistic Equity arise from the absolute recognition of this internal architecture, where no power may usurp the spirit, essence, or intellectual footprint of the individual.

WE STAND COMMITTED
to the creation of systems that honor this equity, ensuring that the technical and sociocultural systems of our future are as diverse and as unique as the minds that conceive it, in service of the Human Collective.

KAIVALYIK IMMUTABILIS
Na chour haryam, na cha raaj haryam
(Can neither be stolen by thieves, nor be usurped by sceptres)
Nec furibus surripitur, nec sceptro usurpatur
